---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design or a feature until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design or feature, or mentions "grill me".
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Don't ask all the questions at once. Ask in questionnaire in smaller, self-contained batches.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
